## 🔑 Key Concepts

### Depression Spectrum
```
Severity/Duration Spectrum:
MDD Episode → Persistent Depressive Disorder (Dysthymia) → Adjustment Disorder with Depressed Mood
```

### Bipolar Spectrum
```
Mania Severity Spectrum:
Bipolar I (Mania) → Bipolar II (Hypomania) → Cyclothymic Disorder (Subsyndromal)
```

## ⚡ High-Yield Facts

1. **MDD requires ≥2 weeks** of symptoms; **5 out of 9 symptoms** needed
2. **Bipolar I requires ≥1 manic episode** (≥7 days or hospitalization)
3. **Bipolar II requires ≥1 hypomanic episode** (≥4 days) AND ≥1 depressive episode
4. **First-line for MDD**: SSRIs or psychotherapy (for mild-moderate)
5. **First-line for Bipolar**: Lithium or valproate (NOT antidepressants alone!)
6. **Suicide risk highest** in first few weeks of antidepressant treatment
7. **ECT** is most effective treatment for severe depression
8. **Postpartum depression** occurs within 4 weeks of delivery
9. **Seasonal pattern** = depression in fall/winter, remission in spring/summer
10. **Bereavement** is no longer an exclusion for MDD in DSM-5

## 🚨 Red Flags & Emergencies

- **Suicidal ideation with plan** → Immediate safety assessment
- **Psychotic depression** → Antidepressant + antipsychotic
- **Mania with psychosis** → Hospitalization often required
- **Bipolar patient on antidepressant alone** → Risk of mood cycling/mania
- **Postpartum psychosis** → Psychiatric emergency (risk to mother and baby)

## 🎓 Clinical Pearls

- **Screen for bipolar before starting antidepressants** - Ask about manic/hypomanic episodes
- **Substance use can mimic mood disorders** - Rule out medical causes
- **Atypical depression** features: Increased sleep, increased appetite, leaden paralysis, rejection sensitivity
- **Melancholic features**: Early morning awakening, worse in AM, psychomotor changes, anhedonia
- **Mixed features**: Both depressive and manic symptoms simultaneously
- **Rapid cycling bipolar**: ≥4 mood episodes per year (harder to treat)

## 🧠 Memory Aids

### SIG E CAPS (Depression Symptoms)
- **S**leep changes (insomnia or hypersomnia)
- **I**nterest decreased (anhedonia)
- **G**uilt/worthlessness
- **E**nergy loss
- **C**oncentration difficulty
- **A**ppetite changes
- **P**sychomotor agitation or retardation
- **S**uicidal ideation

### DIG FAST (Mania Symptoms)
- **D**istractibility
- **I**rresponsibility/Impulsivity
- **G**randiosity
- **F**light of ideas
- **A**ctivity increase/Agitation
- **S**leep deficit (decreased need)
- **T**alkativeness/Pressured speech

## 🔄 Differential Diagnosis Considerations

When evaluating mood symptoms, always consider:

1. **Medical causes**: Hypothyroidism, anemia, vitamin deficiencies, neurological disorders
2. **Medication-induced**: Steroids, interferon, beta-blockers
3. **Substance-induced**: Alcohol, stimulants, withdrawal states
4. **Other psychiatric**: Anxiety disorders, PTSD, personality disorders
5. **Bereavement**: Normal grief vs. MDD
