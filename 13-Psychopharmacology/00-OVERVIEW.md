## 🔥 High-Yield Side Effects to Memorize

### Life-Threatening Side Effects
1. **Serotonin Syndrome** (SSRIs, MAOIs, others)
2. **Neuroleptic Malignant Syndrome** (Antipsychotics)
3. **Agranulocytosis** (Clozapine)
4. **Stevens-Johnson Syndrome** (Lamotrigine, Carbamazepine)
5. **Lithium Toxicity**
6. **QTc Prolongation** (Antipsychotics, Citalopram)
7. **Seizures** (Bupropion, Clozapine)

### Most Commonly Tested Side Effects
- **Sexual dysfunction** → SSRIs (60-70%)
- **Weight gain** → Olanzapine, Mirtazapine, TCAs, Valproate
- **Sedation** → Mirtazapine, TCAs, low-potency antipsychotics
- **Extrapyramidal symptoms** → Typical antipsychotics, Risperidone
- **Metabolic syndrome** → Olanzapine, Clozapine
- **Anticholinergic** → TCAs, low-potency antipsychotics

## 🧠 Quick Reference Tables

### Antidepressant Selection by Clinical Scenario

| Clinical Scenario | Best Choice | Avoid |
|------------------|-------------|-------|
| Sexual dysfunction concern | Bupropion, Mirtazapine | SSRIs, SNRIs |
| Insomnia + poor appetite | Mirtazapine | Bupropion |
| Atypical depression | SSRI (or MAOI) | - |
| Chronic pain comorbidity | Duloxetine | - |
| Seizure history | SSRI, SNRI | Bupropion, Clozapine |
| Pregnancy | Sertraline (safest SSRI) | Paroxetine |
| Smoking cessation | Bupropion | - |

### Antipsychotic Selection

| Clinical Scenario | Best Choice | Avoid |
|------------------|-------------|-------|
| Treatment-resistant schizophrenia | Clozapine | - |
| First episode psychosis | Risperidone, Aripiprazole | Olanzapine (weight) |
| Acute agitation | IM Haloperidol, IM Olanzapine | - |
| Elderly with dementia | Risperidone (lowest dose) | All have black box warning |
| Parkinson's disease psychosis | Quetiapine, Pimavanserin | Typical antipsychotics |
| Pregnancy | Consider Haloperidol | - |

## 📈 Timeline Expectations

### Antidepressants
- **Initial response**: 2-4 weeks
- **Full effect**: 6-8 weeks
- **Trial duration**: 4-6 weeks at therapeutic dose before switching

### Antipsychotics
- **Acute agitation**: Minutes to hours (IM)
- **Positive symptoms**: Days to weeks
- **Negative symptoms**: Weeks to months
- **Trial duration**: 4-6 weeks (except Clozapine: 3-6 months)

### Mood Stabilizers
- **Acute mania**: Days to weeks
- **Prophylaxis**: Ongoing

## 🎓 Study Tips

1. **Make comparison charts** - Side-by-side comparisons help (e.g., all SSRIs)
2. **Focus on differences** - What makes each drug unique?
3. **Learn side effect patterns** - Anticholinergic, serotonergic, dopaminergic
4. **Memorize black box warnings** - Frequently tested
5. **Know monitoring requirements** - What labs to check and when
6. **Practice drug selection** - Given clinical scenario, what do you choose?

## 🔄 Common Drug Interactions

### CYP450 System (High-Yield)

**Inhibitors** (increase other drug levels):
- Fluoxetine, Fluvoxamine, Paroxetine (SSRIs)
- "Fluoxetine Frivolously Inhibits Pharmaceuticals"

**Inducers** (decrease other drug levels):
- Carbamazepine
- St. John's Wort
- Smoking

### Dangerous Combinations
1. **SSRI + MAOI** → Serotonin syndrome (need 2-week washout; 5 weeks for fluoxetine)
2. **Lithium + NSAIDs/ACE-I/Thiazides** → Lithium toxicity
3. **MAOIs + Tyramine** → Hypertensive crisis
4. **Tramadol/Meperidine + MAOIs** → Serotonin syndrome
