## 🎯 High-Yield Concepts

### Legal Frameworks
- **Involuntary commitment criteria**: Danger to self, danger to others, gravely disabled
- **Duration**: 72-hour hold (varies by state)
- **Capacity vs. Competency**: Capacity assessed by physicians; competency by courts

### Risk Assessment Framework
1. **Static factors**: Past behavior, demographics
2. **Dynamic factors**: Current mental state, psychosocial stressors
3. **Protective factors**: Social support, reasons for living

## ⚡ Must-Know Facts

✓ **Best predictor of future behavior is past behavior**
✓ **Suicide completion**: Male > Female (3-4:1)
✓ **Suicide attempts**: Female > Male (2-3:1)
✓ **Highest risk period**: First week after psychiatric hospitalization
✓ **Tarasoff duty**: Duty to warn identifiable victim
✓ **Capacity**: Decision-specific, can fluctuate
✓ **Involuntary commitment**: Requires imminent danger
✓ **Lithium toxicity**: Tremor, confusion, ataxia → seizures, coma
